Step 3 helper — verify weather in-game before moving

1. Turn the in-game clock/weather display on

Use /clock on. On the default UI, the current weather icon is shown by the compass / day-and-time display. XIUI can reposition those HUD elements, so look for the elemental-weather icon associated with that display.

2. Read the icon, not just the sky

Single or double elemental weather both count. Double weather is still the same quest color. For Orange/Clear, you want no elemental weather icon; Light and Dark weather are not substitutes.

3. Remote live checking is limited

From an unrelated zone you generally cannot know the destination's exact live weather. Weather Reporter/Checker NPCs and this forecast tell you what is likely, not what is guaranteed to be active at that second.

4. Use linked zones when possible

Classic linked-weather pairs are especially useful: Selbina ↔ Valkurm, Mhaura ↔ Buburimu, Rabao ↔ Eastern/Western Altepa, and Ranguemont Pass ↔ Beaucedine Glacier. Wait on the safe side, watch the local weather icon, then zone into the eligible outdoor area while it is active.

5. You need to ENTER while the weather is active

The quest credit is checked when you zone into the qualifying outdoor, enemy-populated area. If weather starts while you are already standing inside, zone out and back in while it is still active. Logging out and back in is also documented as a way to retrigger the zone-entry check.

6. What the timer below really means

Forecast rollover ≠ actual weather end. The countdown only tells you how much Earth time remains until the forecast moves from “Today” to the next Vana'diel day. Actual rain, wind, heat, etc. can change within that day.
Best practical workflow: scan → pick a high-probability missing color → if a linked safe zone exists, verify the live icon there first → enter the eligible zone while the icon is active. For Teleport-Dem/Mea/Holla/Vahzl destinations with no remote live check available, use the forecast, teleport in, and immediately confirm the icon.
